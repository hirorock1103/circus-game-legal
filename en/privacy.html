Privacy Policy

日本語

Hiromu Kobayashi (the "Operator") sets out below how user information is handled in the app "Master the Circus" (the "App").

1. Information stored by the App

The App does not save play records or settings. Your BEST level is cleared when you quit the App. Nothing is sent to servers run by the Operator or by third parties. The App has no account registration or sign-in.

2. Automatic network access

At launch, the App checks whether a newer version has been released by looking up the public store listing provided by the app store (Apple on iOS, Google on Android). What is sent is the App's identifier (app ID); as with any network request, your IP address is visible to the store provider. Your name, contact details and play records are not sent.

3. Advertising and analytics

The App shows no ads and uses no usage analytics service. There are no in-app purchases.

4. Deleting information

Deleting the App removes all information stored on your device. Because the Operator stores nothing on any server, there is nothing for the Operator to delete.

5. Children

The App does not collect personal information from users of any age.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
