Master the Circus Support

日本語

"Master the Circus" is a collection of 3D mini games in which you take on three circus acts: the tightrope, the flying trapeze and the human cannon.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Do I need an account?
A. No. You can play as soon as you install the App.

Q. Can I play offline?
A. Yes. The App only goes online at launch, to check whether a newer version is available.

Q. My BEST level is gone.
A. The BEST level is kept only while the App is open. It is cleared when you quit the App.

Q. The screen will not rotate to portrait.
A. The App is landscape only.

Q. Are there ads or in-app purchases?
A. No.
